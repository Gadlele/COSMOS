COSMOS

A drone synthesizer played by the sky. The sun, the clouds, the air pressure, the geomagnetic activity and the moon shape a drone and the instrument above it, and the screen is the real sky above you.

Where it stands

2026-10-26

  • COSMOS 0.16: on the sky alone (LFO, ARP) the keys play without bringing the readings up. Install it.
  • COSMOS 0.15, the splash: at power-on the stars gather into the word COSMOS, hold, and fade into the sky; any button skips it. WiFi starts after it.
  • COSMOS 0.12: HOME after EDIT gives back the real sky at once, the weather and its instrument too.
  • COSMOS 0.11, the real weather: COSMOS joins your WiFi and every half hour fetches the clouds, the pressure and its trend at your coordinates, and the Kp index; the time and your offset from UTC (summer time too) come with them. ENV opens the WiFi page: the networks around, the password on the knobs, WiFi off for offline. Up to six networks are remembered and joined by themselves.
  • COSMOS 0.10, the instruments: one soft drone underneath (FM, sliding to each chord in 1.5 s), and on top an instrument playing the whole chord, attacked anew at each change: ORGAN, STRINGS, CHOIR or WARM PAD. The pressure picks it (high the organ, low the choir), or the settings fix one. The planets give the scale and the chords.
  • COSMOS 0.9, the pads: the drones are now warm analog pads, each note two oscillators a few cents apart, with a slow PWM and a tape wow; each planet its own (SUN PAD, MOON, GLASS, WARM, TAPE, DRONE, HAZE), the moon's waves with none. The night is less muffled, so the chords are heard. The brightest stars stay on the screen even under clouds and by day, faint.
  • COSMOS 0.8, the arpeggiator: a line sweeps the sky and the stars it crosses play the scale by their height, the planets louder; ARP shows it, and what plays lights up. LFO shows the sky alone; the readings stay otherwise. The planets' bells are gone, the chords never stop going round, the buttons in use light up, and saving no longer breaks the sound while you play: COSMOS saves only when you stop it or leave the settings.
  • COSMOS 0.7, the buttons: HOME back to nature, EDIT the whole sky by hand on the knobs, SEL the planets, GLO the settings. The keyboard is one: a key plays the chord on its note (with a planet, the chord of its scale), and plays even with the sky stopped.
  • COSMOS 0.6, the harmony: the seven classical planets, each with its note (TUNING COSMIC, EQUAL or NATURAL) and four chords of its own, one every 10 seconds, in the mode the sun's height gives, coloured by the pressure.
  • COSMOS 0.4, the sky: the sun, the moon, five planets and the 500 brightest stars computed on the FM-1; the screen is that sky, the readings fade in over it on a touch; the sun and the moon play the sound by themselves.
  • COSMOS 0.3, the engine: each drone a small instrument on its own note, a partner that beats against it, its own driven filter, breathing; a planet changes the engine and adds its voice; a tape-like delay, a long reverb, a battery gauge.
  • COSMOS PROBE 0.2, a test firmware (install it): it joins your WiFi, fetches the cloud cover and the pressure (Open-Meteo) and the Kp index (NOAA) over HTTPS, and shows how much memory WiFi leaves for COSMOS's delay and reverb. It makes no sound. First run on an FM-1: connected at −85 dBm, both sources in 386 ms, and room for a 1-second stereo delay beside the reverb. 0.2 keeps the password's big character in lower case when it is.
  • The sound, in your browser: COSMOS's engine sketched in Web Audio, with the five sources on sliders, random weather, the real sky over Rome and the planets' voices. Try it.

The plan and the code: DESIGN · MEMORY · Source

Played by the sky

Every half hour the FM-1 asks the sky how it is, then lets the sound glide there. The slower a source changes, the deeper it reaches: the pressure decides what instrument COSMOS is, the moon how pure its drone, a geomagnetic storm tears it apart now and then, the clouds and the sun colour it through the day.

Moon

29.5 days

The light of the drone. New moon: nearly pure sines. Full: a veil of harmonics, a heavy sub and a singing resonance.

Pressure

hours to days · drastic

The instrument playing the chords. High: an organ. Fair: strings. Falling: a warm pad. Low, rain and storms: a choir.

Kp index

hours · storms are rare

The storms. A quiet sun gives a gentle drift. A geomagnetic storm turns on ring and cross-modulation, and the stars on the screen shiver.

Clouds

hours · medium

The space. A clear sky is dry and near; an overcast one washes the drones in reverb, delay and noise, and hides the stars.

Sun

every day · gentle

The light. Its height opens the filter from the dark of night to the brightness of noon, and follows the seasons by itself.

Planets

while they are in sight

The structure. A planet in the sky can be chosen: its note and its four chords. And the arpeggiator plays the stars and the planets as a line sweeps across them.

A window on the sky

White Terminus text on black, over the stars, the moon and the planets where they are above you now. Touch a control and the readings fade in; leave it and they fade back to the sky.

Playing COSMOS

On a computer with Chrome or Edge and the FM-1 on USB. Installing firmware is at your own risk; the installer puts M-VAVE's own firmware back too. Decline m-bootloader when it is offered: COSMOS does not need it.

  1. Install from the installer. The FM-1 restarts as COSMOS: the readings over the sky.
  2. Connect it: ENV opens the WiFi page. SELECT picks your network, SAVE joins it; for the password SELECT turns through the characters, ALGO jumps between abc ABC 123 #+=, PLAY adds, HOME deletes, SAVE joins. It is remembered (up to six networks) and joined by itself next time. REC turns WiFi off: offline, the weather drifts by itself. ENV goes back.
  3. Set your place (and, offline, the time): GLO opens the settings. SELECT picks a row (latitude, longitude, UTC offset, summer time, date, time, instrument, tuning), ALGO changes it a step, PRESET a big step, GLO saves and goes back.
  4. Nature or your hands: HOME gives everything back to nature: the sun and the moon as they are, the weather as it comes. EDIT puts the whole sky on the knobs: K1 the sun, K2 the clouds, K3 the pressure, K4 Kp, ALGO the moon, PRESET the pressure's trend.
  5. The planets: SEL goes round the seven in the sky (in EDIT all seven), the Sun and the Moon among them, and none. The one chosen gets four corners and plays its note and its four chords (one every 10 seconds). When it sets, it goes.
  6. The sky: LFO leaves the sky alone on the screen, and back. ARP shows the arpeggiator: a line sweeping the sky, the stars and planets it crosses playing and lighting up; ARP again turns it off. The buttons that do something glow, the keys glow on the scale.
  7. The keys: a key plays the chord on its note; with a planet, the chord of its scale for a while, then its four come back. PLAY stops the sky (and saves); stopped, the keys still play. OCT− OCT+ the octave, MASTER the volume.
  8. Back to stock: "Back to the stock firmware" on the installer page.
