How it works
  1. A chord lasts 10 seconds. The one now plays; the next is locked; the ten after it are open.
  2. Tap an open slot and its keys: up to three notes. Each change counts at once; with others writing, wait 15 seconds before the next.
  3. A slot left empty is the sky's: each FM-1 plays its own chord there.
  4. On the FM-1 (COSMOS with RADIO, WiFi on) press FX: it tunes in, and the readings show the chord now and the next.
