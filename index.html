Where it stands

2026-10-26

  • COSMOS 0.2, the first stage: the engine on the FM-1's chip, with the sky in your hands. Each drone is a small instrument: a main oscillator in the moon's character, a partner an octave up that beats against it, a sub, pink noise, driven into its own resonant filter, breathing at its own pace; pitch, cutoff, level and beating all drift. Then a 0.4-second tape-like ping-pong delay, a warm drive over both, and a long diffused reverb. No WiFi and no stars yet. Install it.
  • COSMOS PROBE 0.2, a test firmware (install it): it joins your WiFi, fetches the cloud cover and the pressure (Open-Meteo) and the Kp index (NOAA) over HTTPS, and shows how much memory WiFi leaves for COSMOS's delay and reverb. It makes no sound. First run on an FM-1: connected at −85 dBm, both sources in 386 ms, and room for a 1-second stereo delay beside the reverb. 0.2 keeps the password's big character in lower case when it is.
  • The sound, in your browser: COSMOS's engine sketched in Web Audio, with the five sources on sliders, random weather, the real sky over Rome and the planets' voices. Try it.
  • Next: the sky computed on the FM-1 (sun, moon, stars, planets) and drawn behind the readings; then the weather and the Kp every half hour, and the random weather offline.

Played by the sky

Every half hour the FM-1 asks the sky how it is, then lets the sound glide there. The slower a source changes, the deeper it reaches: the moon and the pressure decide what instrument COSMOS is this week, a geomagnetic storm tears it apart now and then, the clouds and the sun colour it through the day.

Moon

29.5 days · drastic

The instrument's character. New moon: pure, dark sines. Waxing: soft, rounded waves. Full: rich waves, a heavy sub and a singing resonance. Waning: rough, metallic.

Pressure

days · drastic

The harmony between the two drones. High and steady: octaves and fifths. Falling: minor colours and tension. A deep low: tritones and clusters.

Kp index

hours · storms are rare

The storms. A quiet sun gives a gentle drift. A geomagnetic storm turns on ring and cross-modulation, and the stars on the screen shiver.

Clouds

hours · medium

The space. A clear sky is dry and near; an overcast one washes the drones in reverb, delay and noise, and hides the stars.

Sun

every day · gentle

The light. Its height opens the filter from the dark of night to the brightness of noon, and follows the seasons by itself.

Planets

while they are in sight

The structure. A planet on the screen can be chosen, and adds a voice whose pulse comes from its orbit: Mercury glints, Venus bells, Mars a heartbeat, Jupiter a deep swell, Saturn a chime.

A window on the sky

White Terminus text on black, over the stars, the moon and the planets where they are above you now. Touch a control and the readings fade in; leave it and they fade back to the sky.

Playing the first stage

On a computer with Chrome or Edge and the FM-1 on USB. Installing firmware is at your own risk; the installer puts M-VAVE's own firmware back too. Decline m-bootloader when it is offered: COSMOS does not need it.

  1. Install from the installer. The FM-1 restarts as COSMOS, and the drones start.
  2. The sky on the knobs: K1 the sun's height, K2 the clouds, K3 the pressure, K4 the Kp index, ALGO the moon around its cycle, PRESET the pressure falling, steady or rising.
  3. The note: any key moves the drones to its note; OCT− OCT+ their octave. PLAY fades them out and in, MASTER is the volume.
  4. The bottom line shows what the chip spends: the audio's share of its time and the memory left.
  5. Back to stock: "Back to the stock firmware" on the installer page.
