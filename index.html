COSMOS

A drone synthesizer played by the sky. The sun, the clouds, the air pressure, the geomagnetic activity and the moon shape a drone and the instrument above it, and the screen is the real sky above you.

Played by the sky

Every half hour the FM-1 asks the sky how it is, then lets the sound glide there. The slower a source changes, the deeper it reaches: the weather decides what instrument COSMOS is, the moon what the stars sing, a geomagnetic storm tears it apart now and then, the clouds and the sun colour it through the day.

Moon

29.5 days

The voice of the stars, a new one every few nights: drops at new moon, then kalimba, harp, celesta, wind chimes at full moon, glass, a music box, a flute. And the drone's light.

Weather

hours · drastic

The instrument, by day or by night: fifteen of them. A clear night is an aurora of octaves, rain at night strings ringing in sympathy, an overcast day a cathedral organ, a thunderstorm a black hole's pull. The pressure colours the chords.

Kp index

hours · storms are rare

The rhythm. A quiet sun gives a gentle drift. When it stirs, the stars become a Berlin-school sequence; in a geomagnetic storm the whole sky pulses like a pulsar.

Clouds

hours · medium

The space. A clear sky is dry and near; an overcast one washes the drones in reverb, delay and noise, and hides the stars.

Sun

every day · gentle

The light. Its height opens the filter from the dark of night to the brightness of noon, and follows the seasons by itself.

Planets

while they are in sight

The structure. A planet in the sky can be chosen: its note and its four chords. And the arpeggiator plays the stars and the planets as a line sweeps across them.

A window on the sky

White Terminus text on black, over the stars, the moon and the planets where they are above you now. Touch a control and the readings fade in; leave it and they fade back to the sky.

Playing COSMOS

On a computer with Chrome or Edge and the FM-1 on USB. Installing firmware is at your own risk; the installer puts M-VAVE's own firmware back too. Decline m-bootloader when it is offered: COSMOS does not need it.

  1. Install from the installer. The FM-1 restarts as COSMOS: the readings over the sky.
  2. Connect it: ENV opens the WiFi page. SELECT picks your network, SAVE joins it; for the password SELECT turns through the characters, ALGO jumps between abc ABC 123 #+=, PLAY adds, HOME deletes, SAVE joins. It is remembered (up to six networks) and joined by itself next time. REC turns WiFi off: offline, the weather drifts by itself. ENV goes back.
  3. Set your place (and, offline, the time): GLO opens the settings. SELECT picks a row (latitude, longitude, UTC offset, summer time, date, time, pad, stars (AUTO the moon's voice, one fixed, or PAD the pad's own), tuning), ALGO changes it a step, PRESET a big step, GLO saves and goes back.
  4. Nature or your hands: HOME gives everything back to nature: the sun and the moon as they are, the weather as it comes. EDIT puts the whole sky on the knobs: K1 the sun, K2 the clouds, K3 the pressure, K4 Kp, ALGO the moon. PRESET is the drone's volume, always.
  5. The planets: SEL goes round the seven in the sky (in EDIT all seven), the Sun and the Moon among them, and none. The one chosen gets four corners and plays its note and its four chords (one every 10 seconds). When it sets, it goes.
  6. The sky: LFO leaves the sky alone on the screen, and back. SEQ shows the arpeggiator: a line sweeping the sky, the stars and planets it crosses playing and lighting up; SEQ again turns it off. The buttons that do something glow, the keys glow on the scale.
  7. The voyage: ARP opens the world's map. Move the cross with K1 and K2, zoom with K3, choose how long the journey lasts with K4 (1 to 60 minutes), then SAVE. COSMOS fetches the weather there and travels: the clouds, the pressure and the sun's height glide from here to there, the instrument changes halfway, and the sky on the screen dissolves into the sky of the place you are going to. SAVE on the way stops where you are; HOME always brings you home.
  8. The radio: with WiFi on, FX opens the RADIO page and ALGO picks your role. Players pair up at random: one plays the pad (held while the key is), the other the bells, each hearing the other; alone, you play the bells until a partner comes. A listener sees the players on air as a constellation, a line joining each pair: SELECT goes from star to star, SAVE listens. Everything sounds in your own sky's pad and bells. While the radio is on, COSMOS's own sound rests and PLAY sleeps; any other button leaves it. To try it alone, open the test player: it pairs with your FM-1. No FM-1 at hand? COSMOS in the browser is on the radio too, with the FM-1s: its screen, its constellation, its keys lighting up when your partner plays.
  9. The keys: a key plays the chord on its note; with a planet, the chord of its scale for a while, then its four come back. PLAY stops the sky (and saves); stopped, the keys still play. OCT− OCT+ the octave, MASTER the volume.
  10. Back to stock: "Back to the stock firmware" on the installer page.
